# Status and roadmap

HammeRR is in active development.

- **The editor** is usable today for building, texturing and wiring up brush-based maps, by hand or with an AI agent.
- **The compiler is experimental.** Compiled maps load and play in Counter-Strike: Source. Fast and Detailed visibility are available; lighting covers direct light, sky ambient and up to four optional diffuse bounces in independent LDR/HDR channels. CS:S selects the baked LDR or HDR channel according to its HDR setting and hardware support.
