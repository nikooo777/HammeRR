Derived from the 1K glTF asset and its diffuse, normal and AO/roughness/metal
textures. The mesh is centered, rotated to Z up, scaled to 32 Hammer units
high, and converted to clockwise triangles. Globe vertices receive an
editor tint/emission mask. Textures are resized to 512 pixels.

Regenerate with: uv run scripts/import_lightbulb.py
Source downloads are pinned by SHA-256 in that script.
These assets are bundled editor markers, never exported as game models.
